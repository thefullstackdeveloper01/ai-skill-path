FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most about the app, the courses, and how progress and quizzes work.

Is AI Skill Path free?

Yes. There's no subscription or in-app purchase at this time. The app has a reserved ad banner slot on the Home page that's currently switched off.

Why does the app need an internet connection to open, if the content is stored on my phone?

That's an intentional product decision, not a technical limitation. The app checks for an active connection and shows a full-screen "You're Offline" gate with a manual retry when you're not connected, even though the course content itself is bundled locally on your device.

How does lesson locking work?

The first lesson of every module is always open. Later lessons in that module stay locked until you answer the previous lesson's quiz correctly. This applies everywhere you can open a lesson from: the course outline, the in-lesson pager, and search results.

What happens if I get a quiz question wrong?

You'll see a 60-second cooldown before you can retry that quiz. It's there to encourage a re-read rather than repeated guessing.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, your lesson and quiz progress is saved locally on your device.

Can I switch between light and dark mode?

Yes, in Settings. Theme switching is a deliberate toggle and is not tied to your device's automatic light/dark schedule.

Is the app available in languages other than English?

The app currently ships in English only. Nine additional translation files and the switching logic already exist behind a feature flag, so more languages may become available in a future update.

Which platforms is the app available on?

AI Skill Path currently targets Android. If you'd like to know about other platforms, reach out through the contact page.

Do I need any prior technical background?

No. AI Fundamentals assumes no background at all. AI & ML Engineering goes deeper into math and Python, but you can take the first three courses without writing any code.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Use the contact form and choose "Bug report" or "General question" as the topic. Include your device and Android version if you're reporting a bug.

Still stuck?

Reach out and we'll get back to you within 1–2 business days.
