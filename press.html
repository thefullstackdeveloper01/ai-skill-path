AI Skill Path is a mobile learning app offering a structured, four-course path through AI fundamentals, AI platform power hacks, prompt engineering, and AI & ML engineering. Lessons are quiz-gated, so learners must demonstrate understanding before moving forward. The app is free to use and currently available on Android.
