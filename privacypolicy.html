Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information AI Skill Path (the "App," "we," "us") collects, how it's stored, and how it's used. AI Skill Path is a mobile learning app; this policy covers both the app itself and this website.

1. Information We Collect

We deliberately collect very little, because the App is designed to work without an account or personal profile:

  • Local learning progress. Which lessons you've completed and quizzes you've passed is stored only on your device, under a local key (app.completed-lessons). It is not transmitted to us.
  • Local preferences. Your theme choice (app.theme-mode), language selection (app.language), and whether daily reminder notifications are enabled (app.reminders-enabled) are stored locally on your device only.
  • Notification permission. If you enable Daily Reminders in Settings, the App requests your device's notification permission and schedules four fixed local-time reminders (8am, 1pm, 5pm, 9pm). No reminder content or schedule data leaves your device.
  • Connectivity check. To confirm you have a working internet connection (required to use the App), the App periodically sends a small, content-free network request to a public Google endpoint (gstatic.com/generate_204). This does not identify you personally and is used only to detect connectivity.
  • Information you send us directly. If you use the contact form on this website or email thestackdeveloper01@gmail.com, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide, solely to respond to you.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require you to create an account, and does not collect your name, contact details, location, or device identifiers as part of normal use of the learning content. We do not sell personal information.

3. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above to operate the App and this website, respond to support requests, and maintain and improve the course content and app experience. Local data (progress, preferences, reminder settings) is used only to run the App on your own device.

4. Advertising

The App includes a reserved ad banner component and configuration for Google AdMob (native) and Google AdSense (web), currently switched off via a feature flag and configured with Google's public test IDs only. If advertising is ever enabled, this Privacy Policy will be updated first to describe what advertising identifiers or data the active ad network collects.

5. Data Retention

Locally stored data (progress, preferences, reminder settings) remains on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall it. Messages sent through the contact form are retained only as long as needed to handle your inquiry.

6. Data Protection

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any information you send us against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Because most learning data never leaves your device, your control over that data is largely in your own hands.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Since we hold very little beyond what you send us via the contact form, most such requests can be handled by emailing thestackdeveloper01@gmail.com. You can also clear locally stored app data at any time through your device's app settings.

8. Children's Privacy

AI Skill Path is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes, such as enabling advertising, will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to thestackdeveloper01@gmail.com or via the contact page.
