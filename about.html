About Us

We think AI education should look like a path, not a pile of tabs

AI Skill Path started from a simple frustration: the internet is full of scattered AI tips, but very little that takes someone from "I don't really get how this works" to "I can use this well and explain why," in order, on a device you already carry.

Our mission

To give anyone — non-technical learners and future AI engineers alike — a structured, honest, no-hype route through how AI works, how to use it well, and how it's built, in plain professional language with real-world examples.

How we teach

Every course is broken into modules, every module into short lessons, and every lesson ends with a quiz. You can't accidentally skim past the parts you didn't actually understand — the app won't let the next lesson unlock until you show you got the last one.

Our values

What we optimize for

1

Clarity over hype

No breathless claims about AI. We explain what these systems actually do, including their limits, biases, and failure modes.

2

Order over volume

Sixty-two lessons across eighteen modules, sequenced deliberately so each idea builds on the last instead of competing for your attention.

3

Respect for your time

Short lessons, a real quiz gate instead of a "mark as complete" button, and a 60-second cooldown on a wrong answer — enough to make you re-read, not enough to make you give up.

Team

A small, focused team

AI Skill Path is built and maintained by a small team of curriculum writers and mobile engineers. We keep the team lean on purpose — it's part of how we keep the course content current as AI platforms change.

CS

Curriculum & Content

Researches and writes every lesson and quiz, and keeps the platform-specific chapters current as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and others ship new features.

MA

Mobile Engineering

Builds and maintains the Ionic, Angular, and Capacitor app that gets the curriculum onto your phone, including offline handling and progress tracking.

SP

Support

Answers questions, reads every piece of feedback from the contact form, and triages bug reports.
