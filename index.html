How the app works

Built so you can't skip ahead of your own understanding

Every lesson ends in a short quiz. Get it right, and the next lesson unlocks. Get it wrong, and there's a 60-second cooldown before you can try again — long enough to re-read, not long enough to lose momentum.

1

Sequential, quiz-gated lessons

The first lesson of every module is always open. The rest unlock as you prove you understood the last one — in the course outline, the lesson pager, and search.

2

Everything bundled locally

All course content ships inside the app. An active connection is required to use it, but nothing is streamed lesson-by-lesson from a server.

3

Light, dark, or system theme

Switch anytime in Settings. Dark mode is a deliberate toggle, not tied to your device's automatic scheme.
